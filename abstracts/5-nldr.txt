# 5-nldr abstract

This chapter presents non-linear dimension reduction methods. It starts
by reviewing the classical approaches multidimensional scaling, isomap
and local linear embedding. The second section describes two
contemporary methods, t-SNE and UMAP. Assessing the structures observed
in the low-dimensional representation in a visualisation that does
not distort the distribution is important. Section three explains
how this can be done using interactively linked displays: one display
shows the view identified by the non-linear dimension reduction, and
this is linked to a second display that shows linear projections of
the data, ideally a tour display. To illustrate the workflow, the last
section then walks through a more complex example.
