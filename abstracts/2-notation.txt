# 2-notation abstract

This chapter introduces the basic notation used in the book, and shows how
to generate the corresponding objects in R. With the introduced notation,
the mechanics of tours is described, consisting of target selection, target
interpolation and a display of the projected data. Different methods for target
selection are introduced, including random selection (grand tour) and
optimisation based selection (guided tour). The standard interpolation
algorithm is geodesic interpolation, moving along the shortest path between
planes. The available display types depend on the dimension onto which the
data is projected. When projecting onto one dimension, a histogram or dot plot
can be used, in two dimensions a scatterplot display is most natural.
Adjustments such as the slice tour or the sage display can be used in data
with large number of observations or variables respectively.
