# 2-notation abstract

This chapter introduces notation used at various places in the book,  
the corresponding R objects, and the mechanics of tours. Tours are described as
consisting of three elements: target selection, target interpolation and 
display methods. Each of the main methods for each is explained and illustrated, 
including generating targets for grand, guided and radial tours, geodesic 
interpolation, and typical displays or plot types used for different projection 
dimensions, such as histograms and scatterplots. Adjustments to the main methods 
including sage transformation, slicing and constrained projections are briefly 
described.
