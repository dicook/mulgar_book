# 1-intro abstract

This chapter has an explanation about what it means to have a 
high dimensional space, and how to look at high dimensional data 
using low dimensional linear projections delivered by a tour. It 
starts with an intuitive description of tours, and follows with 
some details of how to read axes, and how to detect different 
types of patterns, such as clustering and outliers. There is a 
section on history of the methods and interactive graphics, and 
an example that illustrates what can be learned that would be 
missed without using these methods. An additional section overviews 
the common types of tours and how to use them. It finishes with 
a section noting that these methods may not be needed where there is 
an absence of association between any of the multiple variables.
