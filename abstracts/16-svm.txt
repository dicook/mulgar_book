# 16-svm abstract

This chapter focuses on the specific aspects of model interpreation
for support vector machines and its key elements, the support vectors and
the separating hyperplane. It is first explained how these components can
be extracted, and how to visualise them in the setting with two classes
and two predictor variables. A separate section then shows how we can
examine these model components in high dimensions using the tour to either
explore the support vectors, or to examine the separating hyperplane in
the data space.
